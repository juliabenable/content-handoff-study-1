# Ninety Days, One Surface

Study and prototype for the Benable Brand Portal: where a brand downloads each creator's video and sees when its 90 days of usage rights end.

- `index.html`: the study page (the ask, states, what is agreed, proposed and open).
- `surface.html`: the clickable prototype. The bar at the bottom switches placement, date, tile size, reminder timing and extend flow.
- `calendar-study/`: what other platforms use a calendar for.

Everything in the prototype is PROPOSED unless the study page marks it Agreed. Demo footage and creator names are fictional.

Working notes and the backend research stay in the local folder and are not part of this published copy.
